Observed control-flow excerpts:

1. `dumppreloader` calls `mtk.preloader.init()`, then `mtk.crasher()`, then
   `PLTools.run_dump_preloader(...)`.
2. `dumpbrom` calls `mtk.preloader.init()`, then `mtk.crasher()`, then
   `PLTools.run_dump_brom(...)`.
3. `cmd_stage` calls `mtk.preloader.init()`, obtains a crasher, and uploads
   stage 1 before sending stage 2.
4. `cmd_peek` can parse a supplied preloader and calls `send_da(...)` and
   `jump_da(...)` before reading memory.
5. `mtk_preloader.init()` waits for “PreLoader VCOM”, reads hardware values,
   may send authentication material when DAA is active, and contains device
   register writes in some mode paths.

Therefore “dump” and “read” are not proven non-invasive at the transport
boundary. They can require a crash, payload, DA, authentication, or register
operation before the requested read.
