## Interpretation and limits

`unlocked_ioctl`, `copy_from_user`, a permissive-looking source mode, a policy allow, or a `device_create` call proves source capability or configuration evidence only. A reachable low-privilege sink requires a preserved caller plus node/file-context/SELinux boundary and an observed effect. The sole demonstrated low-privilege driver route is shell → `/proc/ged` query → telemetry; it is not an LPE and does not write HOME, PackageManager, ActivityManager, or Fire Launcher state. The ION/CMDQ/RPMB/input/USB rows remain bounded static or unknown surfaces, with risky operations intentionally rejected.
